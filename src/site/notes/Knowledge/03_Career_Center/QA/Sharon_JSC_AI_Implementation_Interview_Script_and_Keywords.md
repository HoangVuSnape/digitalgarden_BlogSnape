# 🎯 SHARON JSC – AI IMPLEMENTATION SPECIALIST
## Complete English Interview Playbook & Strategy Guide (with Vietnamese Notes)

---

### 01. COMPANY CONTEXT & B2B CONTRACT MANUFACTURING POSITIONING
- **Core Business Model:** Sharon is a **B2B Food Manufacturer & Outsourcing Solution Provider** for hotels, restaurants, café chains, and retail, alongside its artisan bakery brand.
- **Scale & Facilities:** Founded in 2019, 11–50 employees. Main factory: **~1,000 m² in Cát Lái (HCMC)**; 2 service & distribution hubs in **Hanoi** and **Phú Quốc**.
- **Product Lines:** Bakery (baguettes, buns, sandwich bread), Pastry/Viennoiserie (croissants, Danish), cakes, sweets, fine foods, and raw ingredients. Handles both artisanal small batches and mass commercial production.
- **Australian Founder Mindset:** Pragmatic, direct (*no-bullshit*), intolerant of casual tire-kickers. Heavily values **strong ownership, strict deadlines, and clear ROI**.
- **💬 Strategic Opening Statement:**
  > *"From my understanding, Sharon operates primarily as a B2B contract manufacturer in the F&B ecosystem—powering hotels, restaurants, and retail partners with custom bakery products. Therefore, the core AI opportunity isn't just a surface chatbot, but deep automation across demand forecasting, inventory, production planning, QA, and order routing."*

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Nhấn mạnh Sharon là "B2B Contract Manufacturer" (sản xuất gia công cho khách sạn/chuỗi F&B), xưởng Cát Lái 1.000m² và 2 Hub HN/Phú Quốc để gây ấn tượng rằng bạn đã nghiên cứu kỹ công ty.

---

### 02. CORE JOB DESCRIPTION (JD) KEYWORDS DECODED
1. **AI Implementation Specialist:** An applied engineer who integrates existing LLM APIs, agents, and automation tools (Python, n8n, OpenAI/Claude) directly into business operations to cut manual labor—not an academic R&D researcher.
2. **Flagship, High-Priority Programme:** The company's #1 digital transformation initiative, sponsored directly by the Founder with dedicated budget and high visibility.
3. **Map Processes $\rightarrow$ Clear Requirements:** Walking the factory floor and sales desks, documenting current manual bottlenecks (e.g., Zalo copy-pasting), and translating them into technical specifications.
4. **CRM / ERP / POS Integration:** **CRM** (customer records, lead pipeline); **ERP** (flour/butter inventory, production batching, billing); **POS** (retail counter sales).
5. **From Prototype to Release:** Full end-to-end ownership: building rapid prototypes (1–2 weeks) $\rightarrow$ testing with actual staff $\rightarrow$ shipping stable production releases.
6. **n8n / Make:** Low-code workflow automation engines connecting Webhooks and REST APIs across Zalo, Google Sheets, and databases with high agility.
7. **From Office to Production Floor:** Willingness to get hands dirty—stepping onto the bakery floor at Cát Lái to observe ovens, dough prep, and packaging.

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Khi trả lời, dùng các từ khóa như `applied engineer`, `end-to-end ownership`, `map manual bottlenecks`, `hands-on from office to floor`.

### 03. SELF-INTRODUCTION SCRIPT (INTERNAL BUILDER FOCUS)
> *"Hi, nice to meet you. My name is Vu.*  
> *I’m a Software and AI Engineer with a solid Computer Science degree from Ton Duc Thang University, currently completing my Master's.*  
> *At my previous company Innotech, I built backend microservices and agent workflows using Python, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL, focusing on integrating AI capabilities directly into enterprise APIs and databases.*  
> *What really excites me about Sharon JSC is your direct initiative to transform operations with AI. I see myself not just as a coder, but as an **internal builder**.*  
> *I’m comfortable going directly to the operations or sales floor, identifying bottlenecks, and shipping working automations fast with high ownership and strict respect for deadlines."*  

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Nhấn mạnh 3 ý: Bằng CS + Thạc sĩ, kinh nghiệm Innotech làm API/Database thật, định vị là **"Internal Builder"** bám xưởng và luôn đúng tiến độ (*deadlines*).

### 04. HANDLING TECH STACK MISMATCH (SOLID APIS & AGILITY)
> *"I'm 100% comfortable with that.*  
> *To me, technology stacks are simply tools to solve business problems. My foundation in software architecture, databases, and APIs is very solid.*  
> *When you understand system logic and HTTP data flows under the hood, picking up a new framework, language, or low-code tool like n8n or Make takes me just a few days.*  
> *In fact, because I write clean custom code in Python and TypeScript, adapting to visual workflows or existing ERPs is actually much faster. My priority is always delivering reliable output on time."*  

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Khẳng định nền tảng CS và API là gốc; công cụ hay low-code n8n/Make chỉ mất vài ngày để làm chủ; biết code giúp tùy biến tool kéo thả nhanh gấp bội.

### 05. CRM & ZALO EXPERIENCE (VETERINARY SHOP CASE STUDY)
> *"Yes, I actually built a tailored CRM solution from scratch for a real business—a veterinary shop run by my cousin.*  
> *Originally, they bought a 3rd-party software, but it was too clunky, complicated, and didn't fit their daily workflow. On top of that, customer interactions on **Zalo** were overwhelming, leading to buried chats and missed appointments.*  
> *So I stepped in and built a custom web management portal for them. I streamlined customer profiles and linked their Zalo chats directly into the system.*  
> *Whenever a customer reached out, staff could track pet medical history and order status in one centralized dashboard.*  
> *That experience taught me that off-the-shelf CRMs often fail because of complexity, and my role is building lightweight, automated integrations that staff genuinely love using."*  

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Kể case study tiệm thú y: Bỏ phần mềm mua ngoài cồng kềnh $\rightarrow$ Tự build web portal $\rightarrow$ Link chat Zalo vào quản lý hồ sơ tập trung $\rightarrow$ Hiểu tâm lý nhân viên sợ app phức tạp.

### 06. REASON FOR LEAVING PREVIOUS ROLES
> *"At Innotech, my role was a project-based AI engineering internship. I delivered the microservices, LangGraph workflows, and API pipelines as planned, and the contract concluded with great feedback.*  
> *At the university AI Lab, my work focused heavily on academic research. While I learned deep LLM concepts, my true passion is **shipping practical solutions into live business operations** where technology drives direct efficiency.*  
> *That’s why I want to join Sharon JSC full-time—to work directly with leadership on real-world impact."*  

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Innotech kết thúc hợp đồng dự án tốt đẹp; Lab thiên về học thuật; bạn muốn ra ngoài làm sản phẩm thực tế có ROI và giá trị kinh doanh cho doanh nghiệp.

### 07. SALARY NEGOTIATION STRATEGY (8M PROBATION $\rightarrow$ 12-15M+ OFFICIAL)
> *"As we discussed, I am completely fine starting with **8 million VND net during the 2-month probation period**.*  
> *My immediate priority is to hit the ground running, master your workflows, and prove my delivery speed with initial automations.*  
> *However, once I pass probation and demonstrate tangible ROI—such as saving operational hours or eliminating lost customer leads—my expected salary for the official contract is **between 12 to 15 million VND**, with full standard benefits.*  
> *I believe in delivering measurable value first before asking for rewards."*  

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Giữ lời về 8 triệu (gọi là probation base để chứng minh tốc độ bàn giao); sau thử việc review lên 12-15tr+; chốt câu *"Deliver value first before asking for rewards"*.

### 08. REMOTE / HYBRID FLEXIBILITY & HARDWARE SETUP
> *"Given the 6-day work week, my commitment is to be **100% on-site initially** to understand the bakery floor and sales operations thoroughly.*  
> *Once the core pipelines are stable, is there flexibility for **2 to 3 days per month to work remotely for deep-focus coding and system architecture**?*  
> *I ensure 100% output transparency tracked via daily Git commits and progress logs.*  
> *Also, regarding hardware, does Sharon provide workstations/laptops, or is it a BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) setup?"*  

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Cam kết on-site trước để hiểu xưởng; sau đó xin 2-3 ngày/tháng WFH cho mục đích **"Deep-focus coding"**, đo bằng Git commit; hỏi rõ công ty cấp laptop hay tự mang.

> *"In this role, will I be working directly as an individual builder reporting to you, or is there an existing technical team?*

### 10. PROPOSED INTERNAL AI ECOSYSTEM FOR SHARON (4 PILLARS)
*(Sharon seeks an internal operational AI infrastructure across all departments)*:

1. **AI Operations Hub (Unified Dashboard):**
   - Connects CRM, ERP, POS, inventory, orders, production, and finance.
   - Answers operational queries: *"How many baguettes should Cát Lái bake tomorrow?", "Which ingredients are running low?", "Which hotel order is at risk of delay?"*
2. **Forecasting & Production Planning:**
   - Evaluates order history, seasonal peaks, holidays, recurring hotel contracts, and raw inventory.
   - Recommends batch sizes, baking shifts, and procurement needs. **Principle:** *AI proposes, Production Manager approves (Human-in-the-loop)*.
3. **Sales / CRM Copilot (B2B Assistant):**
   - Summarizes client history, drafts quotation emails, tracks expiring contracts, and segments inbound leads from Zalo/Hotline.
4. **Production & QA Assistant (Grounded RAG):**
   - Factory staff queries: *"Standard recipe and bake temperature for Batch X?", "Shelf-life protocol for Item Y?", "HACCP corrective actions for cold storage?"*
   - Strictly grounded via RAG on internal SOPs, Recipes, and QA manuals—zero hallucination.

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Đề xuất 4 trụ cột thực tế cho xưởng bánh: Dashboard điều hành, Dự báo sản lượng nướng (Human-in-the-loop), Copilot cho sales B2B, RAG tra cứu SOP/HACCP xưởng.

### 11. 3-PHASE IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP & ARCHITECTURE

```text
CRM / ERP / POS / Inventory
            ↓
  PostgreSQL Data Warehouse
            ↓
 APIs + Workflow Automation (FastAPI / n8n)
            ↓
 AI Agents + RAG Knowledge Base (Vector DB / LLM APIs)
            ↓
 Executive Dashboard / Zalo / Telegram Alerts
```

- **Phase 1 (Weeks 4–6) – Quick Wins & Foundation:** Clean data schemas, central order/stock dashboard, daily automated revenue digest for Founder via Telegram/Zalo, low-stock alerts.
- **Phase 2 (Months 2–3) – Deep Integration:** Sync CRM/ERP/POS, deploy demand forecasting, automated daily baking schedules, launch Sales Copilot.
- **Phase 3 (Months 6–12) – Full Optimization:** Production & logistics route optimization across Cát Lái, Hanoi, and Phú Quốc; anomaly detection for quality control; cash-flow forecasting.

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Lộ trình 3 giai đoạn: 4-6 tuần ra quick win (báo cáo tự động cho Founder + cảnh báo đơn trễ); 2-3 tháng kết nối ERP/baking plan; 6-12 tháng tối ưu đa hub.

### 12. STRATEGIC PUNCHLINE & REVERSE QUESTIONS FOR THE FOUNDER

#### 💬 High-Impact Business-Minded Closing Statement:
> *"If I were to start, I wouldn't build a fancy chatbot first. I would map the workflows from sales to the kitchen floor, standardize the data schemas, and target a high-ROI bottleneck—such as batch demand forecasting or inventory alerts.*  
> *Initially, AI acts as a **copilot** proposing recommendations for human approval. Once data accuracy and team trust are established, we scale into full agentic automation."*

#### ❓ 3 Insightful Questions for the Founder:
1. *"What is the single biggest operational or sales bottleneck you want AI to solve in the first 30 days?"*  
2. *"Between contract manufacturing for hotels/cafes and retail, which segment is creating the most communication friction right now?"*  
3. *"How does the Cát Lái facility currently synchronize daily orders and stock levels with the Hanoi and Phú Quốc distribution hubs?"*  

> 🇻🇳 **Note:** Câu chốt hạ nhấn mạnh tư duy thực tế: không làm chatbot vô bổ, làm bài toán có ROI trước (copilot hỗ trợ người duyệt). 3 câu hỏi chứng minh bạn hiểu rõ bài toán vận hành chuỗi.
